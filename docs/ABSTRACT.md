We developed and verified a CPU-based motor imagery EEG transfer pipeline with corrected cue alignment, participant-separated source validation and frozen external inference. Across nine BNCI2014-001 source participants, RMS/CSP achieved mean LOSO balanced accuracy 0.696; a fixed label-free participant-alignment variant achieved 0.733. In an authorized provisional PhysioNet evaluation on five participants (225 trials), aligned CSP achieved participant-mean balanced accuracy 0.604 (95% participant-bootstrap interval 0.433–0.766), compared with 0.561 for RMS/CSP. The paired improvement interval included zero, and two aligned participant results were below chance. An earlier exploratory Cho participant result was 0.695. Alignment uses unlabeled target batches rather than strict inductive inference. Frozen model reloads, metric recomputation, repeatability checks and 20 automated tests support technical reproducibility. Prior PhysioNet exposure remains unconfirmed; reliable independent population generalization is not established.
